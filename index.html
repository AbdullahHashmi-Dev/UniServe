UniServe

UniServe is a fictional student-focused tech startup conceived to centralise all essential university services — tutoring, FYP collaboration, ride-sharing, food deals, and task outsourcing — into one intelligent mobile platform. Born from the real pain points of Pakistani university students, UniServe is built on the belief that student life should be productive, connected, and equitable for everyone.

Developed as part of the Introduction to Management course (BSSE), under the guidance of Muhammad Asad Ayaz Khan.
